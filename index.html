Barik Drive Publisher

Barik Drive Publisher is a private, single-user automation operated by its owner. It publishes selected project state and evidence files from the owner's Barebone server to the owner's designated Google Drive workspace.

Google Drive access

The application uses the owner's authorization to list, read, create, update, and verify files required by the publication workflow. It is not offered as a service to other users.

Data handling

Data is not sold, advertised with, or shared with third parties. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the owner's server.
